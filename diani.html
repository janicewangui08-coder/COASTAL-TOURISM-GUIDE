Diani

Location: South coast of Kenya

About Diani

Diani is one of Kenya’s most loved beach destinations, known for soft white sand, clear blue water, and a relaxed atmosphere. It is perfect for visitors who want sunshine, swimming, and a calm seaside escape.

Main attractions

  • Diani Beach
  • Colobus Conservation
  • Shimba Hills National Reserve
  • Nearby coral gardens

Popular activities

  • Sunbathing and beach walking
  • Snorkeling in clear waters
  • Bird watching and nature tours
  • Visiting local coastal markets
