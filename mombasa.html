About Mombasa

Mombasa is one of the most vibrant destinations along the Kenyan coast. It blends beach relaxation with history, markets, and local cuisine, making it a favorite for both relaxing and exploring.

Main attractions

  • Fort Jesus
  • Old Town
  • Nyali Beach
  • Marine parks and coral gardens
