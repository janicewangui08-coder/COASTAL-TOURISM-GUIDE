Lamu

Location: North coast of Kenya

About Lamu

Lamu is a historic island town filled with Swahili culture, narrow streets, and a peaceful atmosphere. It is famous for its timeless architecture and its calm charm, making it a wonderful place for cultural tourism.

Main attractions

  • Lamu Old Town
  • Shela Beach
  • Donkey rides and waterfront views
  • Local festivals and craft markets
