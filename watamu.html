Watamu

Location: North coast of Kenya

About Watamu

Watamu is known for its marine park, coral reefs, and peaceful beaches. It is a perfect place for visitors who enjoy nature, water activities, and quiet coastal scenery.

Main attractions

  • Watamu Marine National Park
  • BioKonzult Reef
  • Arabuko Sokoke Forest
  • Blue Lagoon
